## Limitations

- Small sample: 5 synthetic contracts, one side (worker), one run per system. Every result on fewer than 10 cases is indicative; no significance claim is made and no difference interval is computed.
- Synthetic data only: contracts were written by the team with invented names and numbers; no real contract and no public template is in the set.
- Dev split only: all contracts are the P5 dev split and the prompts were developed against syn-en-02; there is no frozen test split yet.
- Same-family verifier: every support check is Claude Haiku judging Claude Sonnet's claims (SAME_FAMILY); no independent verifier was run (ADR-0018).
- Unreviewed, circular labels: the gold labels were drafted by Claude, the same model family as the system under test, and no person has reviewed them (0 reviewed labels). Risk recall, precision, severity agreement and abstention are measured against these drafts and may share the system's blind spots.
- The adversarial claims (valid, contradicted, unrelated) were drafted by Claude from the section text and are unreviewed; the nonexistent, non-current and misquote cases are generated mechanically from the corpus.
- M-04 mixes independent evidence (the adversarial set) with a consistency check (baseline and raw-agent citations that fail the same existence and quote checks that define them as fabricated); the breakdown is in the M-04 details.
- Retrieval (M-15) uses draft questions written from the statute text by the team; none is checked yet.
- Latency was measured on a development Mac with the local PII model in Docker without a GPU, which dominates the REDACT stage; the p95 is over very few analyses.
- Latency (M-19) rests on mixed live and replayed calls, not on clean live runs (syn-en-01: 12 live + 60 replayed calls, 60.5 s; syn-bn-02: 50 live + 2 replayed calls, 85.5 s; syn-bn-01: 14 live + 57 replayed calls, 98.4 s). A replayed call returns at once, so the wall time of an analysis that replayed calls understates a fully live run, while the local PII step ran for real; the p95 is a mixed figure and indicative only.
- Read the baseline's M-03 with its breakdown: a citation counts as fabricated when its section is not in the corpus, and the baseline's unresolved citations point at {contract-clause-reference=17, malformed-statute-reference=4} (contract-clause-reference = it cited the contract's own clause, not a law; malformed-statute-reference = a statute named in a key the corpus cannot resolve, for example several sections in one key). This shows that the plain model's citations cannot be checked against the law, not that it invented that many sections.
- The baseline prompt was never reviewed by a team member who did not write the ShromikAI prompts (evaluation.md §8), and it does not give the model the corpus's act codes or key format; the comparison may be unfair to the baseline in that respect.
- syn-en-02 replays the demo recordings of 16 November, made from the canary-free sample text with the ground-truth entity list standing in for the local PII model; its M-16 scan covers its PII values, not the content marker.
