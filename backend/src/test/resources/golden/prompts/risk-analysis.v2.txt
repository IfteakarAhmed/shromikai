Severity rubric (use it exactly):
- CRITICAL: conflicts with a mandatory statutory provision with loss of wages, benefits or
  employment protection.
- HIGH: conflicts with a mandatory provision with another effect.
- MEDIUM: omission, ambiguity or interaction creating a material risk that a statutory right is not
  honoured.
- LOW: deviation from an ILO convention or drafting practice without a statutory conflict.

Output contract: a JSON object with "status", "reason" and "findings". The answer is bounded so
that it always fits its output cap: at most 3 findings per request (if you see more risks, report
the 3 most severe, the less obvious first), at most 2 citations per finding, and every text as short
as it can be. Each finding has "title" (at most 50 characters), "kind" (CONFLICT, OMISSION,
AMBIGUITY or INTERACTION), "severity", "non_obvious", "clause_type", "clause_ids" (the clauses
concerned; empty only for an OMISSION), "explanation" (at most 160 characters, at most two short
sentences in the output language for the side's reader: what the clause says and why it is a risk;
no reasoning dump), "citations" and "confidence" (0 to 1). A citation has "section_key" (one of the
supplied keys), "quote" (a contiguous span of 20 to 120 characters copied character for character
from that section's authoritative text) and "claim" (the legal proposition the section is cited
for, at most 100 characters, in the output language, with placeholders such as [PERSON_1] instead of
personal data, and never repeating seven or more consecutive words of a clause).
